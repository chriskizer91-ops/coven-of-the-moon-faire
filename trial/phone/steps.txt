A TRIAL FOR YOUR PHONE: can one game page open the next by itself?

The finished game will be a few pages (one for each zone). When the coven flies to the
next zone, the page you are on has to open the next page by itself, and your save has
to come with it. Nobody knows yet whether your phone allows that for downloaded pages.
These two tiny pages find out. Each is a few KB. Nothing is fetched from the internet.

Steps (about two minutes):

1. Download both files, trial-1.html and trial-2.html, into the SAME folder on your
   phone (your Downloads folder is fine). Keep their names as they are.
2. Open trial-1.html from that folder, the way you open a game build.
   It says whether it could keep a made-up save.
3. Tap the big button "Open page 2".
4. If page 2 opens: it shows three answers, each YES or NO. Tell the hub the three
   answers (a screenshot is best). It also says what kind of address it was opened
   as (for example "file" or "content"): send that too.
5. If page 2 does NOT open (an error, a blank page, or nothing happens): tell the hub
   what you saw. Then open trial-2.html yourself from the same folder and tell the
   hub its three answers.
6. "Back to page 1" on page 2 tries the way back. Tell the hub if that worked too.

If you can, do the same on the laptop: it tells us the laptop's answers are as
expected (all three YES).
