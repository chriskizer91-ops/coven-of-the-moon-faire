# zone-4: the northeast peaks, the last zone

Branch: job/zone-4      Updated: November 24, 2026

A sub-agent of the hub's wrote this, building zone 4 the way zones 2 and 3 were built. Nobody could be asked while it
worked, so every choice it made is listed under "The job's calls", each with the other choice.

**The one rule above the others was kept: no word of `game/talk/zone-4.js` was changed.** Its 144 lines are being
recorded as they stand. Every one was read against the land before anything was placed, and again at the end
("Every line, against the land", below).

## What it built

### 1. The land

- **`game/pages/zone-4.html`**: the page, made from `zone-3.html`, with the menu job's marked block kept.
- **`game/places/zone-4.js`**: five of the old Envoi's places on one land, 3.1 km by 1.9 km of Chris's map: the frozen
  camp (where the ship sets down), Frostmere's shore, the frozen pass, Misthollow and the dead Moonwell.
- **Four roads join them: 3.3 km of walking from the camp to the Moonwell, climbing 256 m.**

  | Road | Length | It climbs |
  |---|---|---|
  | the frozen camp to Frostmere's shore | 1,726 m | 117 m |
  | Frostmere's shore up to the frozen pass | 481 m | 65 m (the steepest: 17 m in every 100, in a cutting through deep snow) |
  | the frozen pass up to Misthollow | 1,048 m | 48 m |
  | Misthollow's street up to the Moonwell | 60 m | 3 m |

- **Misthollow is joined to the land** (it was a town with two doors). From the south, a bridge of stone setts on tall
  posts carries the road over the sea of mist to the foot of the town's south stair. To the north, its one street runs
  on between the houses, out of the town and up to the Moonwell.
- **No flame is lit in Misthollow or at the Moonwell.** The lines say so four times. The old places had warm lanterns,
  lit windows and two fire bowls: here every window is dark, every wall lantern is gone, all thirteen lamp pillars
  stand with a dead lantern on them, and the fire bowls are cold iron.
- **The light up there is a pale cold dawn** ("never dark, never warm", "it's always morning here"): a bright,
  colourless sky and a white mist, in those two places only. The road up is night, as in zones 1 to 3.
- **At the frozen camp, made new:** the Grey Hat's ship on the landing ground, with twelve lamps lit on her rails and
  a spare on a post by her stern; and the pass-keeper's hut, 47 m east along the trail to the pass: three plank walls,
  a roof under snow, and an iron stove with its door glowing.

**And on the land, the things the lines speak of:**

- **The forty Wardens.** Two files of twenty up Misthollow's street, from its foot to the edge of the town, each
  facing up the street to the Moonwell, the middle of the street left clear. They are still copies of the
  night-watchman's shape in his long coat (the "cloaks" of the lines, until Wardens are painted). Nobody among them can
  be talked to until the fire ends.
- **The great bramble**, across the road in the cutting 72 m above the frozen pass: 83 black canes, frost on their
  tops, from one bank to the other. It cannot be walked through.
- **Halcyon**, on the top step of the Moonwell's stair, in a tall white light. She is not a person to talk to: all
  twelve of her lines are said inside the coven's set moments.
- **The cold lights**: twenty-three small pale lights. Sixteen drift up the road, over the bridge, up the street and
  into the dead well; five pass the pass-keeper's hut and never stop; two sit by Ede's door.
- **The fire**: it burns white in the Moonwell's two bowls; when the letter is given it rises in the dead well.

## Where a fight belongs (there are none on this page yet)

1. **The great bramble**: on the road named "the frozen pass up to Misthollow", 72 m along it, in the cutting. The spot
   is `W3D.ZONE4.bramble.at`. The coven's lines before it are the moment `frozen-road`. When it is won the story needs
   the flag `bramble` set (`story.flag('bramble', true)`): the canes over the road go and the road opens. Once
   `window.__fights` exists on the page the stand-in stops setting that flag by itself.
2. **The fire, in two forms**: at the dead Moonwell, the zone's gate fight. The story looks for it as
   `window.__fights.gate` and counts it won by `party.gates[id]`, as in zones 1 to 3; its name here is `theFire`
   (any other works); its spot is `W3D.ZONE4.fire.at`, the well. The coven's lines before it are the moment
   `before-fire`. **Between its two forms the fight must play the moment `between` itself**
   (`window.__story.moment('between')`): the stand-in plays it only while there is no gate.
3. Lines that wait on a fight just won or lost (the Grey Hat's and the watchwoman's, two each) are not heard yet.
   The narrator's `woke` and `not-ready` lines for zone 4 are written and not wired, as in zones 2 and 3.

## The job's calls (each is one line of data; each is Chris's to overrule)

In `game/places/zone-4.js`:

1. **The peaks stand at one ninth of the map's height, not one third** (`VK`). Measured first: at one third the
   camp is 41 m up and Misthollow 775 m, and no road gentler than 18 m in 100 exists between them shorter than
   7.8 km, in cuttings up to 87 m deep. At one ninth the climb is 256 m and the roads are 3.3 km. The fog hides
   everything past 80 m, so what is lost is not seen. *The other choice:* one third, with a road more than twice as
   long. (Anything between works too: it is one number.)
2. **Misthollow stands 143 m south of its pin** (`MIST_PIN`), on the flat top of its mountain. On the pin itself the
   town would sit on the mountain's north rim, with the land falling away where its street has to climb.
3. **The dead Moonwell stands at the head of Misthollow's street, 153 m north of the town** (`AT.well`), which is
   290 m south of its own pin, and 12.8 m higher than the town's lower square (`WELL_UP`). The lines need it there:
   "The Moonwell's up there, where the street ends", "the cold lights go up the street to the Moonwell". On its own
   pin it would be 300 m away across open mountain and 47 m *below* the town. *The other choice:* on its pin, with a
   780 m road winding down to it, and three lines untrue.
4. **The other three places are on their pins.** The mountain is steep under them (the map is up to 48 m above
   Frostmere's shore's own ground at its edge, and 60 m above the frozen pass's), so the roads leave them in cuttings
   through the snow. Zones 2 and 3 have the same (up to 44 m). Moving each 200 to 270 m would halve it; not done.
5. **The flames** (`flameless`) and **the dawn** (`DAWN`, and `DARK` for after the moon has set).
6. **The ship at the frozen camp is the Skiff** (`SHIP`), the only ship baked for lying at a landing. By the hub's
   reading the ship flown in from the northern wilds is the Brig: when she is baked
   (`node tools/flight-bake-ship.mjs brig`) and her file is loaded in the page, she takes the Skiff's place by herself.
7. **The pass-keeper's hut stands 47 m east of the fire, on the trail to the pass** (`HUT`). *The other choice:* at
   the frozen pass itself, 2 km on, where nobody would be sent to her from the landing.
8. **Names a player reads on the land are stand-ins:** "The frozen road", "The snowbound pines", "The northeast
   peaks" (the landing's name), and the herb "White sage" (Sorrel sells it).

## What had to be made new for snow and ice country

- **Nothing in the engine.** The land wears the places' own snow, trodden snow, frozen rock and ice.
- **A sky** (`game/places/zone-4-sky.js`). The first three zones have one night sky with no stars. Zone 4's is that
  same sky with three things added: the pale dawn over Misthollow and the Moonwell; the stars; and a moon that sets.
- The dawn's light and mist (numbers only).
- The bridge into Misthollow (the place engine's own deck, posts and rails) and the bank of rock it lands on.
- The dead lanterns (the engine's own lantern, with no flame); the hut and its stove (the engine's own parts).
- **Still copies of a body** (`bake` and `crowd` in `game/people/zone-4.js`). A person as the game makes one is 23 to
  31 separate pieces to draw. Forty would be about a thousand. The forty Wardens are two bodies, each copied twenty
  times as plain numbers and joined: 33 pieces and 110,000 triangles for all forty.

## Every line, against the land

All 144 were read against what is built. **None had to be reported as untrue.** The ones that leaned hardest on
the land, and what makes each true:

- "This is as high as she goes. Up there, no flame stays lit": the camp is the lowest place, 256 m under the Moonwell;
  every flame in Misthollow and at the Moonwell is out; the pass's one iron lamp is the last lit one on the way up.
- "Twelve lamps lit and a spare", "I'll light a spare", "Good thing I lit the spare": twelve on the ship, and the
  spare, dark until he says it.
- "I keep the stove lit", "The cold lights never stop at my stove": the stove, and five lights that pass it.
- "at the bottom of Misthollow's one street", "by the dead lamp", "Up the pass, past the bramble": so.
- "Forty-one went up. Forty are here", "Forty Wardens stand in the street": forty, counted by the check.
- "The cold lights go up the street to the Moonwell... Nobody comes back down": they go up only.
- "The Moonwell's up there, where the street ends. Someone stands on its stair", "On the top step", "The light on the
  stair's gone out": so.
- "Look. Moths. Hundreds. Thousands": 1,400. "The moon's setting", "I'd forgotten the stars", "Look at that dark": so.
- "Down. They're all coming down", "It caught. My fire caught", "Footprints. Coming down. Forty pairs", "You came
  down. Five of you": each has its thing on the land after the fire.

**Three lines the land cannot show, none of them wrong:**

- `z4.europa.03` ("Someone mended their cloaks. Badly."): the forty wear the night-watchman's long coat, with no
  mending on it.
- `z4.europa.11` ("Captain. Your lamps aren't leaning."): no flame leans anywhere in the game (the board already
  lists it for the whole game).
- `z4.sorrel.07` ("A green leaf, on my white sage"): no leaf is drawn.
