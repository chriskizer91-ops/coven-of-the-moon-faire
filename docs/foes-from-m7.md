# Foes from Aethermoor M7: a suggestion list for Coven of the Moon Faire

Written for the hub by a sub-agent that only read (Opus 5.5, extra high), and looked over by the hub. The fights job
should read it before filling a creature slot.

For Chris. Written November 23, 2026, from M7 as it stands at commit `2e549e0`. Nothing was copied or changed in
either repo. **Every pick below is a suggestion. Which creatures come in, and where, is your call.**

Pictures, one sheet per zone, made from your own model sheets in M7, went to you with this list on November 23
(`zone-1.jpg` to `zone-4.jpg`). They are not kept in this repo: nothing of M7's is copied in until a creature is
wanted in the game.

## 1. What M7 has

- **93 foes planned:** 54 creatures and 39 people. You have painted a model sheet for all 54 creatures.
- **29 creatures built in full detail:** a sculpt of 0.6 to 1.3 million triangles shaped to your sheet, baked onto a
  light model of about 14,000 to 52,000 triangles that moves. 28 pass M7's no-stretching check; the thornhound does not yet.
- **24 more built in code from rigid pieces** (11 wraiths, 10 iron and forge ones, 3 winged), first to third pass.
  Rigid pieces cannot stretch.
- **1 creature only planned:** the rime wolf (your sheet, no model yet). The 39 people are made by M7's hero builder.

## 2. Suggestions, zone by zone

How to read the tables: **A** and **B** are the two slots the fights job keeps in each zone (A is on, B is off). Each
slot has a job in a fight, from the fights job's step 4 on the board. "Phone set" means the light model plus its
smallest pictures: what a phone would load. I measured those myself. Triangle counts are from M7's own files.

M7's places and this game's are pinned on the same map: your atlas of Aethermoor, counted in the same pixels. That
gives a plain test for "lives here". On that map:

- **Zone 1's land** holds M7's Thornhollow (this game's Wickhollow), the Thornway (the Thornwood), Fawnrest (Io's
  cottage), the Hearth Road, the Murkway, Willowmurk, Bogmire and the Lanternfen (the fen's dark heart).
- **Zone 2's land** holds M7's Hindwood and Eldergrove.
- **Zone 3's land** holds M7's Drystone Lea, near the crossroads. M7 also dresses its own Eldergrove with the same
  3D place as zone 3's Eldergrove's edge.
- **Four of zone 4's five pins** are M7's Last Camp, Frostmere, the Rockslide Pass and Peak's Veil (with the
  Highfold), on the very same pixels. The moonwell's pin has no M7 place of its own.

## 3. Good, but they do not fit here

- **The desert ones** (sand-skink, glass-scorpion, the Glass Matriarch, the Sand Wyrm, Kharzul): this game has no desert.
- **The iron and forge ones** (Mother Anvil, forge-spark, iron-sentinel, the Sentinel-Captain, the Ashen Warden,
  forgeborn, bellows, the Journeyman, forge-warden, cinder-thrall, thrall-overseer, the Unsmith, the unmade): dwarf
  halls, forges and the deep under the Keep. None of this game's four zones is that country.
- **The bosses and named beasts** (Briarmaw, the Rotwarden, the Lantern Mother, the Rime-Abbot, the Drowned Abbess,
  Grandfather Willow, the Blackwater Leviathan, Old Jaws, Gorrow the Mire-King, the Rot-Stag, Old Snag): too big for a
  slot that comes about one fight in five. Each is a story boss in M7. The Lantern Mother's home is this game's
  fen's dark heart, with the same lamp story, so she would compete with the great wraith.
- **The moths** (lamp-moth, the Gloamwing): here a pale moth is a freed soul. A moth as a foe would muddle that (guess).
- **The drowned of M7's fen** (the Drowned, the Bell-Ringer, the Drowned Choir, the Drowned Cantor): their homes are
  the Long Boardwalk and the sunken "Misthollow Ruins" in M7's fen. That is a different place from this game's
  Misthollow on the peaks. The rime-wraith already covers the drowned-monk idea.
- **The Wisp-Queen:** the desert's Miragewell, and a Relic-Bearer, too big for a slot.

## 4. What bringing one in would take (facts from the files, not a plan)

**What a built creature is in M7.**

- **The full-detail kind** is a folder, `prototypes/foe-hd/foes/<name>/assets/`, holding:
  - the light model (`light.bin`, compressed, 0.2 to 0.5 MB for these picks);
  - `light.json` (its bones);
  - its pictures (colour, surface, shading, glow, sometimes shine) at 512, 1,024 or 2,048 px. The phone sets
    measured 0.5 to 1.1 MB;
  - the sculpt itself (1.8 to 6.4 MB). It is for looking at; a game does not need it.
- **It is moved by M7's code:** the kit's loader (`kit/js/loader.js`) and a moves file for its body plan
  (`kit/js/moves/willow.js`, for example). The walkers' moves are in `pages/walkers/foes.js`.
- **The rigid-piece kind** (the wraiths, the winged) is not a file at all. It is built in code when the page loads,
  from M7's hero kit (`prototypes/hero-3d/`) and its sculpted-pieces picture (about 0.7 MB), plus `wraiths.js` or
  `winged.js`.
- **Both kinds use three.js 0.170**, loaded as modules. This game runs **three.js r128**, as one script
  (`game/lib/three.r128.min.js`). Whether M7's loader runs on r128 was **not checked**. My guess is it needs changes:
  it uses newer three.js features.

**What a foe has to be in this game's battle** (`game/battle/models/wisp.js`, `screen.js`, `fights.js`, `rules.js`).

- **A model maker** that returns:
  - its root and effects;
  - `play(name)`, plus whether it is busy and how far along;
  - a list of its actions, each with the moments its blows land;
  - anchors (where a blow hits it), a fade, a guard, and a reset.
- **The actions the screen asks for by name:** hurt, block, die, appear, stagger, plus each of its moves.
  `screen.js` already has a way for "a new mob" to play its moves from its model's own motions (`anyMove`).
- **A look line** in `fights.js` (its height, shadow, glow, how near it strikes from).
- **A foe entry** in `rules.js` (HP, speed, weaknesses, experience, and each move's blows and targets).

**What this means.**

- **The numbers do not carry over.** M7 fights with dice: an intent die from d6 to d20, and HP of 10 to 215. This game
  uses big HP (a wisp 1,100, a wraith 2,600) and turn gauges. Only the look, the motions and the move names come
  across. The fights job would set new numbers.
- **The two shapes differ** (M7's `pose(move)` against this game's `play(name)` and action list), so something would
  have to join them. How much work that is, I could not tell without trying.
- **The fights job's slot file is not in `main` yet** (the board: "nothing pushed since the morning"). I could not
  read how a slot names its model.
- **The rules on both sides:**
  - Here (`CLAUDE.md`): copy only what the game uses, one line each in `assets/INDEX.csv`, and nothing loaded from
    M7 when the game runs.
  - M7 has been a read-only source here since November 23, 19:40, and nothing is copied until you want a creature.
  - M7's own rule: a foe that fails the stretch check does not go into a game.
- **Size:** this game's zone pages are already 87 to 100 MB. One creature's phone set is about 1 MB.
- **Not checked at all:** how any of these look or run in this game's battle, or their frame rates. There is no
  screen here. M7's slips say their pages were checked in a browser with no graphics card. The slips I read do not
  say these picks were checked on your phone.
