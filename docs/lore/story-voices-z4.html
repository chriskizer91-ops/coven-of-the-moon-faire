What passed

  • All 144 lines are here, each once, named exactly as the order asked, and nothing extra.
  • The words shown beside each recording on your listening page match the lines word for word.
  • The format is right on all 142: MP3, one channel, 44.1 kHz, 64 kbps.
  • The loudness is even: -18.9 to -17.1 LUFS against the asked-for −18, and no peak above −1 dBFS (the highest is -1.0).
  • The silence at the start is never more than 0.07 s. At the end, 2 files run a hair over the asked-for 0.2 s (ede.02 0.21 s, watch.05 0.21 s). Not worth redoing.
  • No line is in the wrong voice, as far as pitch can tell: every speaker’s lines sit in their own range. The lines that stray are mostly meant to: shouted calls, excited hellos, quiet asides.
  • No line looks cut short or padded with extra words. The longest against their estimates are lines with a cue or a slow tone; the shortest are quick calls.
  • All 144 together are 9 minutes 4 seconds, about 4.3 MB (my estimate was 9.7 minutes).
