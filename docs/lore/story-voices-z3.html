What passed

  • All 124 lines are here, each once, named exactly as the order asked, and nothing extra.
  • The words shown beside each recording on your listening page match the lines word for word.
  • The format is right on all 124: MP3, one channel, 44.1 kHz, 64 kbps.
  • The loudness is even: -18.8 to -16.8 LUFS against the asked-for −18, and no peak above −1 dBFS (the highest is -1.2).
  • The silence at the start is never more than 0.08 s. At the end, 2 files run a hair over the asked-for 0.2 s (captain.12 0.21 s, gil.01 0.23 s). Not worth redoing.
  • No line is in the wrong voice, as far as pitch can tell: every speaker’s lines sit in their own range. The lines that stray are mostly meant to: shouted calls, excited hellos, quiet asides.
  • No line looks cut short or padded with extra words. The longest against their estimates are lines with a cue or a slow tone; the shortest are quick calls.
  • All 124 together are 8 minutes 14 seconds, about 3.9 MB (my estimate was 8.5 minutes).
  • The returning voices are the same voices: Io 188 Hz here, 202 in zone 1; Kore 222 Hz here, 219 in zone 1; Europa 258 Hz here, 267 in zone 1; Callisto 200 Hz here, 196 in zone 1; the Grey Hat 132 Hz here, 140 in zone 1. Within a few per cent, which is as close as one voice gets from day to day.
