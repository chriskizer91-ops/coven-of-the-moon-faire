  • The words shown beside each recording on your listening page match the lines word for word.
  • The format is right on all 236: MP3, one channel, 44.1 kHz, 64 kbps.
  • The loudness is even: -18.9 to -16.7 LUFS against the asked-for −18, and no peak above −1 dBFS (the highest is -1.1).

  • No line looks cut short or padded with extra words. The longest against their estimates are lines with a cue or a slow tone; the shortest are quick calls and the Grey Hat (below).
