# Zones 2 to 4: who speaks, how much, and what for

**Status: a proposal by the story job (Claude), November 19, 2026. Not approved.** An outline only, from the story
page Chris approved (`story-page.md`). No line here is written yet. Lines are written when the hub asks, zone by zone,
in the same shape as zone 1 (`game/talk/zone-1.js`). Where this outline and Chris disagree, Chris wins.

## What the three zones are for

- **Zone 2, the Warm Roads and Dawnroost (levels 5 to 10).** The cold. The fire drinks the warmth out of the roads,
  and Io learns that a tended flame is not a held one. At Dawnroost, the Wardens' hall still makes up forty beds for
  the Wardens who went north with her sister.
- **Zone 3, the northern wilds and the shipyard (levels 10 to 15).** The knight. A knight of light comes down the north
  road and fights with Io's own spells. At the shipyard the shipmaster remembers her, and says her name. Io learns her
  sister is alive.
- **Zone 4, the frozen pass, Misthollow and the dead Moonwell (levels 15 to 20).** The letting go. Misthollow is never
  dark and never warm, and nobody there has grieved in eight years. On the Moonwell stair, Io gives her sister the
  oldest letter by hand. The fire ends, the moon sets, and the game ends back at the Faire.

Every zone keeps zone 1's shape: five stages the game keeps, people who point the coven on by places it can find, a
few people with many lines whose small stories the zone is told through, and the four witches at set moments.

**The coven's moments:** the landing; the first dark node; the first node lit again; the forty beds; before the node;
after the node (Io folds her letters for the first time, and they take wing); the leaving.

**The small stories**

- *The roadwarden.* Begins: puts a hand on the cold road stones and asks the four to light the nodes. Turns: admits
  he stayed behind because he was afraid, and has kept the road alone eight years. Ends: the road warm under his hand
  again; he will keep it, through the dark.
- *Marta.* Begins: "Little Io," and the forty bunks, made up every night. Turns: she asks Io whether she should stop.
  Io, who holds on, cannot tell her to. Ends: she keeps making them, "until somebody tells me different". (It pays
  off at the very end, if call 14 is yes: the forty walk home.)
- *The Grey Hat.* The cold eats his sunstone. He flies low and hates it. He does not complain. Much.

**The threads in zone 2:** Io relights the nodes with small flames she tends, and learns a kept fire is not a bad
fire (tending is not holding). Kore looks at Marta's forty bunks and cannot see one of the forty's endings, and does
not say so. Europa sees the warmth itself going north along the threads, and sews the nodes shut against the pull.
Callisto walks the cold road like someone who has done it before.

**The coven's moments:** the landing; the shipyard; Eldergrove's edge; before the knight; after the knight (Kore:
she has no ending); the name; the leaving.

**The small stories**

- *Ysmera.* Begins: passages sold by the berth, no sentiment. Turns: the four ask about the north, and she goes quiet.
  Ends: she remembers the paladin who came through her yard, and says the name. She gives the four something of the
  moon's for the road (a moon-glass, as in the new Envoi).
- *The last passenger.* She has her passage. Each time the four come back she has not gone. At the end she gives
  it to someone else.
- *The Grey Hat.* The moon-folk offer him a great deal of money for his ship. He does not take it, and will not say
  why.

**The threads in zone 3:** Io meets her own spells in a stranger's hands, then learns whose. Her hope is now the thing
she holds hardest. Kore, at the crossroads, cannot seal the knight, and says the line the zone is built round: "She
has no ending." She understands what the fire does, and is frightened for Io. Europa sees every thread in the north
run to one knot, and the knight tied into it. Callisto sees the knight first, coming down the road, and is the one who
says to wait.

**The coven's moments:** the landing (the Grey Hat stays); the frozen road; Misthollow; the stair; the finale (before,
between the two forms, after); the moon setting; the ending at the Faire.

**The finale, beat by beat** (from the approved ten lines): Callisto holds the one moment. Europa, who sewed the
flames down, cuts the threads that hold each light. Kore draws the fire's hunger onto herself and gives it its ending.
Io sends her letters at last, and each held soul follows its own name home. Eight years of moths go up at once. One
small flame is left, the one Halcyon carried from home, and it is kept.

**The threads in zone 4:** Io lets go: the letter by hand, the rest sent, and home left again, saying goodbye this
time. Kore defends: she takes everything the fire has, so Io does not have to. Her bargain comes due (call 13).
Europa learns some cloth has to be cut. Callisto says it aloud, to Halcyon: it is a season.
