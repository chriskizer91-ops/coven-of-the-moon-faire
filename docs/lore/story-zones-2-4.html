What the three zones are for

  • Zone 2, the Warm Roads and Dawnroost (levels 5 to 10). The cold. The fire drinks the warmth out of the roads, and Io learns that a tended flame is not a held one. At Dawnroost, the Wardens' hall still makes up forty beds for the Wardens who went north with her sister.
  • Zone 3, the northern wilds and the shipyard (levels 10 to 15). The knight. A knight of light comes down the north road and fights with Io's own spells. At the shipyard the shipmaster remembers her, and says her name. Io learns her sister is alive.
  • Zone 4, the frozen pass, Misthollow and the dead Moonwell (levels 15 to 20). The letting go. Misthollow is never dark and never warm, and nobody there has grieved in eight years. On the Moonwell stair, Io gives her sister the oldest letter by hand. The fire ends, the moon sets, and the game ends back at the Faire.

Every zone keeps zone 1's shape: five stages the game keeps, people who point the coven on by places it can find, a few people with many lines whose small stories the zone is told through, and the four witches at set moments.

The small stories

  • *The roadwarden.* Begins: puts a hand on the cold road stones and asks the four to light the nodes. Turns: admits he stayed behind because he was afraid, and has kept the road alone eight years. Ends: the road warm under his hand again; he will keep it, through the dark.
  • *Marta.* Begins: "Little Io," and the forty bunks, made up every night. Turns: she asks Io whether she should stop. Io, who holds on, cannot tell her to. Ends: she keeps making them, "until somebody tells me different". (It pays off at the very end, if call 14 is yes: the forty walk home.)
  • *The Grey Hat.* The cold eats his sunstone. He flies low and hates it. He does not complain. Much.

The threads in zone 2: Io relights the nodes with small flames she tends, and learns a kept fire is not a bad fire (tending is not holding). Kore looks at Marta's forty bunks and cannot see one of the forty's endings, and does not say so. Europa sees the warmth itself going north along the threads, and sews the nodes shut against the pull. Callisto walks the cold road like someone who has done it before.

The small stories

  • *Ysmera.* Begins: passages sold by the berth, no sentiment. Turns: the four ask about the north, and she goes quiet. Ends: she remembers the paladin who came through her yard, and says the name. She gives the four something of the moon's for the road (a moon-glass, as in the new Envoi).
  • *The last passenger.* She has her passage. Each time the four come back she has not gone. At the end she gives it to someone else.
  • *The Grey Hat.* The moon-folk offer him a great deal of money for his ship. He does not take it, and will not say why.

The threads in zone 3: Io meets her own spells in a stranger's hands, then learns whose. Her hope is now the thing she holds hardest. Kore, at the crossroads, cannot seal the knight, and says the line the zone is built round: "She has no ending." She understands what the fire does, and is frightened for Io. Europa sees every thread in the north run to one knot, and the knight tied into it. Callisto sees the knight first, coming down the road, and is the one who says to wait.

New calls for Chris (my pick first)

  1. Where the game ends. Pick: back at the Faire's camp outside Wickhollow, the night the moon sets. The Grey Hat flies the four home with Halcyon; the fair folk say goodbye; the Faire moves on, and the four with it. (The hub would need one last flight and the camp by night.)
  2. Kore's bargain comes due. She was told below: walk the lost home, and you may take your bow. At the finale the lost go home. Pick: she is offered her bow, and does not take it. "Not yet." The one who always let go chooses to stay, for Io.
  3. The forty Wardens. Pick: forty Ember Wardens went north with Halcyon. The fire holds them, standing, in Misthollow. They walk home at the end, and Marta's forty bunks are for them.
  4. What the knight says at the crossroads. Pick: two words, "Go home", and nothing that gives her name. Ysmera names her afterwards, from her own memory of her yard.
  5. New people, known by their trades only: the roadwarden and the carter (zone 2), the bramble-cutter and the last passenger (zone 3), the pass-keeper and a held Warden (zone 4). No new name is spoken. Pick: yes.
  6. The Grey Hat cannot fly to Misthollow, because no flame stays lit there and he does not fly dark. At the very end, under the first stars, he flies dark again. Pick: yes.
  7. What the brambles are is never said in a line. People guess, as they do about the wisps. Pick: yes.

The ship's name stays unsaid (call 7).
