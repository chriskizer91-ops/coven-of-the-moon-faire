## Why these two

- The Grey Hat says of the peaks: "Up there, no flame stays lit." The fire at the dead Moonwell calls every light
  to itself. The Wickstoat lives on that pull: it pinches any flame it finds and carries it up toward the well.
  When it steals from the coven, one more spine on its back lights up.
- The Hoarmantle is the mountain's own beast, older than the trouble. Misthollow has been "never dark, never warm"
  for eight years, so the frost on its back has never melted. It is not wicked. It is in the road, and it is huge.
- Both walk on four legs, like the Thornhound and the Rot-Stag, so they can be built and moved the way M7's
  beasts already are. One is small and quick and low; the other is the biggest thing in the game's wilds.
